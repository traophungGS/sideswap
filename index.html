Swap Sides

  • Each side has three counters. Red starts on the left column, yellow on the right. Red moves first.
  • On your turn, slide one counter along a line to the neighbouring empty point.
  • The two halves connect only through the middle row, so the counters have to get past each other there.
  • The first side to put all three counters on the opposite end column wins. With jumps on, you can also hop over an adjacent counter to the empty point right behind it.
  • If you have no legal move, your turn passes.
